Questions

Common questions

Do you charge for quotes?

No. Quotes are free and there’s no obligation to go ahead.

Which areas do you cover?

We’re based in Mitcham and work across South London and Surrey, including Mitcham, Wallington, Sutton, Croydon, Morden and the surrounding areas.

Do you only do roofing?

No. As well as roofing we do guttering, fascias and soffits, brickwork, carpentry, garage conversions, garden rooms, driveways, patios, plastering and decorating.

Should I get my roof checked before winter?

It’s a good idea. Small problems like slipped tiles, cracked mortar or blocked gutters are much cheaper to fix before the colder, wetter weather turns them into leaks.

How do I tell if water is getting in around my chimney?

Look for damp patches around the chimney breast, water marks on ceilings near external walls, cracked or missing mortar, and signs of water after heavy rain.
